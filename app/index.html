Roadmap stage 1 of 8 · story first

One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude asks you to meditate at 18 spots across Leonida — and the Chapter 1 balcony spot is reported to disappear after Chapter 1, with no mission replay and no New Game+. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 manual save. The community has not confirmed any other missable trophy — see the Missables tab.

Roadmap to platinum

Eight stages in play order, built only from the official descriptions and the cited sources. Story first, then strangers, then the open-world work — and the 100% cleanup last, because A Long Road is the gold that usually lands last.

Missables

Exactly one trophy is reported as potentially missable. Everything else can be earned after the story ends.

What we do not know yet

PlanetGTA6 (2026-11-19) is blunt about this: there is no reliable list of missable trophies yet, because the game has only just come out. The Chapter 1 meditation spot is the one the guide community — KeenGamer, OutputLag, GTAVibes — agrees on. Beyond it, this guide claims nothing. Keep manual saves before the big story missions anyway; it costs nothing and covers what the community is still confirming.

Sources & method

Every guide in this entry is written in our own words. A fact that comes from somewhere else names its source beside it — a cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed. Nothing here copies another guide's text.

Honesty notes

  • Rockstar has never published an official trophy list on its own channels. The list here reached the public through the PSN tracker databases — Exophase and PSNProfiles, cross-checked with TrueAchievements — and was reported by PlanetGTA6 on 2026-11-18.
  • The 100% completion contents are unpublished. No guide can honestly list them, so the roadmap is built from the official descriptions only.
  • Play times are estimates from reviews (about 60 hours for the story). They are not facts.
  • Cheats are reported by PowerPyx to disable trophies, and GameSpot and PC Gamer report that you cannot save after using one. Rockstar has published nothing on GTA 6 cheats — so never use a cheat on your trophy save.
  • There are 0 online trophies. The Online filter exists because the competition asks for it, and it honestly shows an empty set.
  • Xbox has the same 55 achievements, worth 1,000 Gamerscore in total; A Long Road is the biggest at 100G.
