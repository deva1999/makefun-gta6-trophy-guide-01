        Ground rules for a clean run

        • Keep manual saves. Save at every chapter boundary, and keep a Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops. There is no mission replay and no New Game+ to go back with.
        • Never use cheats on your trophy save. PowerPyx reports that turning on a cheat disables trophies, and GameSpot and PC Gamer add that you cannot save afterwards. Rockstar has published nothing on GTA 6 cheats — this is reported, not confirmed — but the GTA 5 precedent (Rockstar Support: codes temporarily disabled trophies) says the same thing.

        Honesty notes

        • No Rockstar-published list. Rockstar and Sony have never published an official trophy list for GTA 6. The 55 names, descriptions and grades here are the PlayStation list as published on Exophase on 2026-11-18, cross-checked with PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements.
        • Hidden flags follow the visible / hidden split published by PlanetGTA6: 27 visible, 28 hidden. The 28 hidden trophies are the story chapters, the named missions, the stranger finales and the spoiler-y open-world moments.
        • 100% completion is unpublished. Rockstar has not said what A Long Road’s 100% covers. The in-game stats page is the checklist; any claimed task list (including a circulating “35-task” figure) is unverified.
        • Times are estimates. Reviews put the story at about 60 hours; the road to platinum is commonly estimated near 200 hours. Both are estimates, never facts.
        • Counts come from PSN. Where a trophy has a number — 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 100 WAiNK It locations, 18 meditation spots, 8 stranger strands, 3 card sets, 5 vaults, 50 challenges, 20 races, 100 vehicles, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages — the number is PSN’s published progress target.
        • Unconfirmed triggers are labelled. A few trophies have deliberately vague official descriptions (Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Challenging Tank, Catch Me If You Can). Their guides say the trigger is not yet confirmed instead of inventing one.
